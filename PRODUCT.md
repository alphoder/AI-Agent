# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday people who want to speak better out loud — primarily **students and people building general confidence** (public speaking, social/dating nerves, class presentations), and beyond them job-seekers, non-native English speakers, and professionals. They arrive a little nervous, often on a phone, with little patience for setup. The job: *get talking fast, feel safe practising, and see that I'm improving.*

## Product Purpose

SpeakCoach is a free, voice-only AI speaking-practice app. You sign in with Google, pick (or get recommended) a scenario, and have a real spoken conversation with an AI coach. Afterward you're scored on what you said (rubric) and how you carried yourself (body language, read live, never stored). Bixy — a friendly blue voice assistant — can run the whole app by voice.

Success = a nervous first-timer finishes a real spoken session in their first few minutes, comes back tomorrow, and can *see* their confidence climbing.

## Signature concept (phased)

1. **Adaptive journey** + **Bixy as the spine** (now): a personalized path that reacts to your goal and scores — "Day N of your path", streaks, a next-best drill — with Bixy as the always-there guide.
2. **Live real-time coaching** (next): a calm on-screen read while you speak — pace, filler words, confidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, confident, encouraging. Restraint over flash (Linear/Things, not a gamified toy). It should feel like a quietly expert coach: warm enough to calm nerves, precise enough to trust. Voice: plain, kind, direct. No hype, no jargon.

## Anti-references

Cartoonish gamified language apps; saturated multi-colour SaaS dashboards; anything loud, busy, or "techy." No purple anywhere. No gradient text, no neon, no confetti-by-default. Not an enterprise LMS (that's the past life we pivoted away from).

## Design Principles

- **The tool disappears into the task.** Every screen has one obvious next action; talking is never more than one tap away.
- **Show progress, not features.** Surface streaks, scores, and the journey — proof they're improving — over feature tours.
- **Calm beats clever.** One blue accent used as a scalpel; greys and white carry everything else. Quiet by default, expressive only at real moments.
- **Safe to be bad at first.** Encouraging copy, gentle empty/first-run states; never punish a low score.
- **Phone-first, dead-simple.** Big tap targets, legible text, no setup walls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body/secondary text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, visible focus rings, keyboard-operable controls. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Voice is the primary modality, but text transcripts and tap alternatives exist for every voice action. Webcam body-language analysis is opt-in and never stored.
